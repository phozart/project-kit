## Example Persona

### Sarah the Startup Founder

**Photo**: Professional headshot, casual business attire

**Quote**: _"I need tools that just work. I don't have time to read manuals."_

---

#### Demographics
- **Age**: 32
- **Location**: San Francisco, CA
- **Occupation**: Founder & CEO, B2B SaaS startup
- **Education**: MBA
- **Tech Savviness**: High

#### Context
- **How they found the product**: Recommended by investor
- **Frequency of use**: Daily
- **Devices used**: MacBook Pro, iPhone
- **Environment**: Office and remote, often on the go

#### Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal**: Efficiently manage product development and team coordination
- **Secondary Goals**:
  - Track project status without micromanaging
  - Collaborate with distributed team
  - Make data-driven decisions quickly
- **Success Criteria**: Ship features on time, keep team aligned, minimize overhead

#### Pain Points & Frustrations
- **Current Pain Point 1**: Too many tools, context switching is exhausting
- **Current Pain Point 2**: Hard to get visibility into what's happening without constant meetings
- **Current Pain Point 3**: Project management tools are either too simple or too complex
- **Biggest Frustration**: Wasting time on admin work instead of strategy

#### Behaviors & Preferences
- **Preferred interaction style**: Quick and efficient, values keyboard shortcuts
- **Decision-making style**: Data-driven but trusts gut for people decisions
- **Learning preference**: Self-service with video tutorials
- **Communication preference**: Slack for urgent, email for updates

#### Needs from the Product
1. Single source of truth for project status
2. Quick setup with minimal configuration
3. Integrations with existing tools (Slack, GitHub, etc.)
4. Mobile access for on-the-go updates

#### Potential Objections
- "Another tool? We already have too many"
- "Will the team actually use it?"
- "Is the pricing sustainable as we scale?"
